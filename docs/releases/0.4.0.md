# 0.4.0: resume, progress and clear errors in the car

Needs an Artifact MCP server whose viewer shell supports `media.progress` (ADR-0022,
artifact-mcp PR #114) for progress, chapters, buffering and the page's error reasons. With an
older server the app works as 0.3.0 did.

## New

- **Resume.** Play with nothing loaded (headset, Bluetooth, the car's play button, the system's
  resume card) resumes the newest Continue item; the page continues at its saved place. Android
  Auto's "recent" root is answered with that item. With the app stopped, a media button starts
  the media service only when resuming can play.
- **Progress.** Now playing shows the position and length of the current chapter (or the whole
  reading), interpolated between updates. Continue shows how much of each artifact is played.
- **Chapters.** Artifacts with chapters (e-reader pages) get a queue with one item per chapter.
  Picking one, or next/previous, jumps to that chapter.
- **Forward 30 s** next to Back 15 s. Speed moves to the overflow menu when both are shown.
- **Buffering.** While Listen loads, restores its place or reconnects, the car and the
  notification show buffering instead of playing.

## Fixed

- A pick without a network failed only after a 45 s spinner. It now fails at once with
  "No connection", and Continue and Library mark their rows "Needs a connection".
- Every failure was reported as a timeout. Errors are now distinct: no connection, the artifact
  is gone, server error, sign in (with an "Open on phone" action), and the page's own reason
  (for example "Speech is unavailable"). Play after a failure tries again.
- A voice search with no match failed silently. The car now shows why (no match, no library,
  signed out, offline).

## Protocol

`ready.capabilities` adds `media.progress`. `media.session` accepts `state: "buffering"`,
`position`, `duration`, `chapterIndex`, `error` and the actions `forward` and `chapter` (at most
12). New message `media.chapters`. The app sends `media.command` `forward` and `chapter` with
`index`. Unknown fields are still rejected.

## Permissions

Adds `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (install time) and an exported media button receiver
(`ResumeButtonReceiver`) for playback resumption.

## Not tested in a real car

Android Auto on a phone and the Desktop Head Unit are not tested: its resume card, error screen
with the "Open on phone" button, queue view and the placement of the third custom button.
Tested: JVM unit tests, the phone emulator (viewer and Listen/car library through the legacy
media browser API) and the Android Automotive OS 15 emulator media center.
